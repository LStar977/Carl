## Description (≤4000)
```
Stop filling out the same application 300 times. Carl is the AI that finds you a job — and prepares every application for you.

Carl reads your résumé, learns what you want, finds your best-fit roles, and writes a tailored cover letter and answers for each one. Then Carl opens the real application page, fills in your details, and you tap Submit. You're in control the whole way — and you only pay when an application actually goes out.

WHY CARL

• Applications that actually go out. No bot quietly failing in the background. Carl prepares each application and you confirm the send — so it really sends.

• You only pay when it sends. Pay per application, never a subscription. Your credits never expire and there's no weekly quota you lose.

• You approve everything. Review and edit every résumé, cover letter, and answer before it goes to an employer.

• Great-fit, not spray-and-pray. Every match comes with a fit score and the reason Carl picked it, so you apply where you actually belong.

• Try it free. Your first 3 applications are on us — no card needed.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Add your résumé — or let Carl build one from scratch.
2. Tell Carl what you want: role, pay, city, remote or on-site.
3. Carl finds your matches and writes each application.
4. Review, then apply in one tap. Employers reply right to your email.

Built for job seekers in the United States and Canada.

Carl is not affiliated with, and does not guarantee placement with, any employer or job platform. Pricing is per application via in-app purchase; your first 3 are free.
```
