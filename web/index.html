Questions

Good to know.

How does Carl apply for me?
Carl opens the employer's real application page right inside the app, fills in your details, and hands you your tailored cover letter and answers. You review and tap Submit. Because you confirm the send, it actually goes out — no bot quietly failing in the background.
Will I pay for applications that don't send?
No. A credit is spent only when you confirm an application went out. No charges for failed submissions, no monthly quota that expires at the end of the week — your credits are yours until you use them.
How do I hear back?
Employers reply directly to your email, exactly like a normal application — interview requests land in your inbox, just as if you'd applied yourself. Carl keeps a record of everything it applied to inside the app.
Where does Carl work?
Carl supports the United States and Canada to start. We're expanding from there.
Is my résumé private?
Yes. Your résumé is used only to find and apply to jobs on your behalf — it's never shared with third parties or sold.
What does it cost?
You pay per application using credits, spent only when an application actually sends. Your first 3 are free (no card needed), credit packs start at $19, and credits never expire. No subscription.
